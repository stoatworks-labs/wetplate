# AGENTS.md — Wetplate

Onboarding for whoever (or whatever) picks this up next. `CLAUDE.md` is the short
command reference; this is the *why*. Read "What is actually verified" before you
tell anybody this works.

---

## What the plugin is

A collodion wet plate — blue-sensitive and seconds long — as an FFGL 2.1 effect
(`WT01`, shown as `SW Wetplate`) for Resolume Arena and Avenue. C++17 + GLSL 4.10,
CMake, universal macOS `.bundle` and a Windows `.dll`. MIT, intended for
`github.com/stoatworks-labs/wetplate`.

Built 2026-09-24 in one session (tranche four) from the fleet's templates and
`specs/SPEC-wetplate.md`: rebate for a photochemical process with a characteristic
curve, the plugin shape, the harness and verify; afterglow for a ring of frames;
photofinish for the resize-mid-run trap; toner for the `--pipe` mode with SIGPIPE
ignored; tinsel for `PassBuffer`, the sweep and CI.

## The one idea

**The plate, not a grade.** Two properties of a wet collodion plate make its look
and both are in the plate: it sees only ultraviolet and blue, and it is slow.

| the stage | what comes out |
| --- | --- |
| a sensitivity curve ending at 500 nm, integrated against each pixel's spectrum | **reds and skin dark, skies white**: red exposes 7% of white, green 2%, blue 98% |
| a sliding box of `Exposure` seconds, as a ring of bucket sums | **motion is a ghost** with density exactly the fraction of the window a thing stayed |
| a take that integrates from an event and then holds | **a capped lens**: the developed plate |
| thin-film drainage from the pour corner, h ∝ √s | **pour marks**: the drain corner heavy, the pour corner thin |
| a seeded pour edge; dust the silver bath ran round | **the bare edge; specks and comets** with tails down the drain |
| the silver image over black, or a negative on light | **tintype, ambrotype, negative** |

The pipeline, in order:

1. **Expose.** The host's frame, sRGB-decoded to linear, through Smits' rule: the
   reflectance of an RGB is a sum of seven basis spectra (white, cyan, magenta,
   yellow, red, green, blue) chosen by the order of the components. The plate's
   exposure to each basis under the chosen light is precomputed (`Spectral.h`), so
   a pixel's exposure is the same seven-term sum. Times the frame's seconds, ADDED
   into the current bucket (GL_ONE / GL_ONE on an R32F buffer).
2. **Sum.** H = Σ buckets in the window × (1 / seconds in the window). The window's
   arithmetic is all on the CPU in double; the shader sees sixteen weights.
3. **Coating.** h = 1 + V(√s − 2/3) along the drain, times the bare-edge mask and
   the defects. Cached: rendered again only when something it depends on moves.
4. **Plate.** H × 2^Sensitivity × the vignette → log10 → the curve
   `c(x) = (sp(x − Toe) − sp(x − Toe − L)) / L` → D = h(Fog + γLc) → the silver
   intercepts a = 1 − 10^−D → mix(plate, silver, a), or silver × (1 − a) for the
   negative → sRGB → Mix. Alpha 1.

### What falls out, and what does not

- **The ratios are not tuned.** Red at 7% and green at 2% of white are what the
  digitised curve says against Smits' basis; nobody chose them. Under tungsten the
  balance shifts a little toward green (4%), because the light has more of the
  plate's long end.
- **The ghost's density is exact.** Each column of a smear reads (frames covered) /
  (frames in the window), to float rounding — the harness measures it.
- **The window is exact to one bucket's width, and no better.** That is the price of
  holding B frames of memory instead of every frame in the exposure. `--bucket`
  measures the bound and shows it is reached.
- **Not modelled:** the ultraviolet (a clip's pixels say nothing about it, and the
  basis carries none), grain (collodion is very fine; below a pixel at video
  rasters), the collodion's own colour, intensification, halation, a real fluid
  pour, the plate's varnish. Skin has never been seen through it.

### The ring, precisely

`Buckets` slots, each an R32F frame holding Σ(exposure × dt) over the frames it
took. A grid of cells `Exposure / Buckets` seconds wide, anchored at host time 0
(Continuous) or at the take (Take). A frame at time t goes to cell
`floor(t / width + 1e-9)`; when the cell changes, the ring advances one slot, which
is cleared and opens at the cell's start. Per slot the CPU keeps `start` and
`seconds`, in double.

The window: a slot is in it if `start ≥ now − Exposure − 1e-9` (Continuous) or
always (Take). In steady state that is every slot, so the window runs from the
oldest slot's start to now — between Exposure − width and Exposure long. Weight =
1 / Σ seconds of the slots in the window, 0 otherwise; H = Σ slot × weight. A
clock jump of more than a cell leaves the skipped cells' slots older than the
window, so the rule drops them on its own.

A structural change (Buckets, Mode) resets the ring. An Exposure change does not:
the grid changes width under the old slots, which keep their recorded starts and
seconds and fall out of the window by the same rule.

## Traps

Roughly in the order they will bite.

### ☠️ A float round-trip of a control puts the grid a frame late

`--ghost` first read a window of 29 frames where 30 were expected, and `--take`
found the plate still changing on the frame it should have held. Both because
`Exposure` 0.5 s went through `ExposureParam` → float → `ExposureSeconds` and came
back as 0.50000002 s: the cells were 0.1000000004 s wide, and by the sixth
boundary `0.6 / width` fell to 5.9999998 and floored to 5. In a host the exposure
is whatever the slider says and nobody notices; in a harness that expects a frame
to land in a cell, one frame moves. The law is now 1/16–16 s over 0..1 so every
multiple of 1/8 is a power of two exactly (0.375 is 0.5 s, 0.5 is 1 s). Found by
tracing `now`, `dt` and the cell per frame (`WETPLATE_TRACE=1`).

### ☠️ `InitGL` cleared a take that had already been pressed

The sweep reported `Take` dead. Its context delivers the press through `--set`,
before the first frame — which is before `InitGL`, and `InitGL` reset
`takePending`. A host presses after InitGL, so `--take` (which presses at frame 20)
never saw it; only the sweep, which does everything before the context exists,
did. A press is a press: InitGL leaves it alone now.

### ☠️ The fog floor read as a milky mid grey on every black

The first look at Resolume's demo clips had every black background at sRGB 0.4:
a fog of 0.065 density on the tintype is a = 0.14 of the silver over the plate,
which is a grey. The fog law is now 0.004–0.030 (0.012 at the default); the
plate's blacks are the plate. Same session, the one-second default turned every
fast VJ loop into streaks; the default is half a second, with 16 s on the slider.

### ☠️ The bench had the 4K upload on the clock

11.3 ms at 4K, of which 10 were `glTexSubImage2D` of a fresh 33 MB card per frame.
A host's frame is already on the GPU. The card is uploaded once now, like the
siblings; the plugin's cost does not depend on what the frame holds, because every
pass runs every frame and the coating is cached whatever the picture does. 1.1 ms.

### Output alpha is 1, on purpose

Resolume's demo clips are DXV with alpha, and an effect that passes `src.a` through
comes out transparent wherever the clip is (toner's release found it). A plate is
opaque: the plate pass writes alpha 1 whatever the source's was, including under
Mix.

### Inherited from the fleet, and all still true here

`ScopedFBOBinding` does not restore the viewport (the host's is captured first and
put back before the plate pass); every `ffglex::Scoped*` clears to 0 on exit, so
every `Ensure()`, the resize resample and every bucket clear happen before anything
binds a texture — and the sum pass binds all sixteen units by hand and unbinds them
after; `FFGLFBO::Release()` leaks the colour texture, which is why
`PassBuffer::Destroy()` deletes it first; `SetParamInfo` clamps a STANDARD default
into 0..1 before `SetParamRange` can widen it; the core is an **OBJECT** library;
`SetTextParameter` must return `FF_SUCCESS` for the About block; the harness drives
a synthetic clock; `nm | grep -q` fails under pipefail when grep succeeds; an
option's range reads back 0..1 whatever its element count; Resolume's clock
overflows a float, so every time here is reduced in double on the CPU and the
shaders see only `Dt` and weights; a reallocated buffer is a cleared buffer, so the
buckets are resampled and swapped on a resize, never reallocated in place; `packed`
and the rest of GLSL 4.10's reserved words are not identifiers (verify greps for
them); no `M_PI`, no `far`, no `near`.

## Would this hold on another rasteriser, at another raster?

One line per check. Every tolerance is derived, not fitted; every check ran at
320×180 and 1280×720 in `verify.sh`. kU = 2^−24, half a float ULP at 1. The raw
checks read H through the `Probe` hook as floats, so no encode is involved; the
density checks read the Negative view at Tone 0 through the sRGB encode, and the
read error `kDensityRead` = 1e-5 is ten times what the exp2 (3 ULP, GLSL 4.10 §8.2),
the encode's pow (~9 ULP) and the curve's summed terms can account for (≈ 1e-6).

| check | what it measures | tolerance and where it comes from | raster dependence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--spectral` ratio | H of a pure red, green, blue patch over H of a white one, each light, from the Probe | **2(n + 4) kU** relative, n = 20 frames: n adds of one float and a product, each half an ULP, for each operand of the ratio | none: patches are whole bands, read at a band centre |
| `--spectral` white | H of white | (n + 4) kU of 1; white is `Basis[0]` = 1.0f exactly by the script | none |
| `--spectral` physics | red, green < 0.15; blue > 0.8 of white | coarse by design: the spec's claim as inequalities on the computed weights | none |
| `--ghost` window | buckets held, seconds held | exact count; 1e-9 on seconds (double bookkeeping) | none |
| `--ghost` smear | every column of the smear vs (frames covered) / n, n = 30 | **(n + 4) kU** absolute (E = 1 exactly: decode(1) = 1, white basis 1) | none: the square moves 2 WHOLE pixels a frame, so coverage is integer and translates exactly; a fractional speed would be a different check |
| `--ghost` static, black | the static square = 1; black = 0 | same; black **exactly 0**: 0 × Dt summed is 0 and 0 × weight is 0 | none |
| `--bucket` bound | H vs the exact 30-frame box mean on every frame of a 31-frame run | **(wb − 1) / n = 5/30**: the frames the box has that the oldest bucket has dropped; |box − H| ≤ e/n with e ≤ wb − 1, from the two sums' bounds | none (whole-pixel motion) |
| `--bucket` reached | max |box − H| > 0 | the bucketing is real, not hidden by an exact window | none |
| `--bucket` rule | H vs Σ over the stated window / m | **(n + 4) kU**: the same float sum | none |
| `--curve` steps | D at 41 step centres vs the stated curve, three Developments | **kDensityRead** = 1e-5, above | none: the wedge is a float texture and steps are sampled at centres; ≥ 2 px a step |
| `--curve` floor, ceiling | D far under the toe and far over the shoulder | the softplus residual γL/Knee e^(−Knee d) at d = 1.5 and 1.3 (< 1e-4) + 2 read | none |
| `--curve` secant | slope between the steps nearest 0.4 inside each bend | **[γ(1 − e^(−Knee da) − e^(−Knee db)), γ] ± 2 read / span**: the mean-value theorem on the curve's own derivative bounds; loose (0.98γ) because the latitude is short | none |
| `--drainage` fit | D/D₀ along the drain axis vs a + b√s, four pours | residual ≤ **2 × 2 read / D₀** = 5.1e-5: two densities per point, each read to 1e-5, over D₀ ≈ 0.75 | s is computed from the pixel centre by the stated projection at whatever raster; 21 samples |
| `--drainage` law | a = 1 − 2V/3, b = V | 4× the per-point read (1e-4) | none |
| `--take` | 0 before; changed on all 29 exposing frames; bit-identical from frame event + n | **exact**: no accumulation runs, so the sum pass reads the same buckets with the same weights | none |
| `--resize` | every pixel = 0.5 after white, 1.5× up, black, back down | **(60 + 8) kU**: 60 adds and a product, plus two bilinear resamples of a flat field (weights sum to 1 within an ULP); measured 0 | the resample is bilinear, exact on a flat field at any ratio; a textured field would be a different (softer) claim |

Deliberately NOT relied on: `pow(1, x) == 1` (white is tested within a tolerance),
`mix(a, b, 1) == b` (the plate pass returns early at Mix 1), exact cancellation
anywhere, implicit derivatives (the shaders take none), or the input texture's
filter (the expose pass uses `texelFetch`).

What might still differ on another rasteriser: the resample pass is a filtered
read, which on a flat field is exact but on a picture is the driver's bilinear; the
bare edge's `smoothstep` and the defects' discs are antialiased by arithmetic and
so identical everywhere, but nothing checks them beyond the sweep.

### The negative controls

`wttest --negative` runs seven, and `--perturb BITS` runs any check verbosely against
one. Each perturbs the *plugin's* model — a `Perturb` bit the shipped plugin carries
at zero — never the harness's expectation.

| perturbation | what fails, measured at 320×180 and 1280×720 |
| --- | --- |
| weight by BT.709 luma instead of the plate | `--spectral`: 9 of 14 (every ratio, every light; red 0.21 against 0.074) |
| integrate one frame instead of the window | `--ghost`: the window holds one bucket; the smear is 20 columns at 1, not 78 |
| integrate one frame instead of the window | `--bucket`: the difference from the box mean reaches 0.83 against a bound of 0.17 |
| coating linear in s instead of √s | `--drainage`: the √s fit's residual is 0.03 against 5e-5, all four pours |
| γ × 0.8 in the shader | `--curve`: every step off by up to 0.3 in density; the secant 0.90 against [0.98, 1.20] |
| a resize that clears the buckets | `--resize`: every pixel reads 0 against 0.5 |
| a take that never stops integrating | `--take`: frame 50 differs from 49 |

### The mutation

One character of the shipped GLSL, on a clean committed tree: in the coating
pass, `sqrt( s ) - 2.0 / 3.0` → `sqrt( s ) + 2.0 / 3.0` (a coating whose mean is no
longer 1). Caught by `--drainage`: a = 1.53 against 0.20 for every pour, the residual
untouched — which is right, the profile is still √s. Nothing else depends on the
coating, so nothing else failed. Reverted with `git checkout source/Shaders.cpp`; the
tree was clean before and after.

## Decisions taken without asking

- **White normalises to 1 under every light.** `Light` changes the balance between
  colours and never the overall exposure: a photographer sets exposure for the light
  there is, and a `Light` menu that dimmed the picture would be a bad control.
- **Planckian lights.** 6504 K for daylight (the CCT of D65, which is not Planckian),
  2856 K for tungsten (Illuminant A's definition), 5500 K for flash. Said in the
  script and in ATTRIBUTIONS; the CIE daylight tables were not typed from memory.
- **The sensitivity is the 40/60 bromide/iodide curve** of Hertel et al., digitised
  from their Figure 4, because it is the recipe the authors chose as the historical
  optimum. Read off the plot to ±0.03; tapered to zero at 520 nm past the 500 nm
  the text gives. The UV is not represented.
- **Smits' table could not be re-fetched** in the session; the transcription is
  checked by the complement property (pairs sum to white within 0.072). The script
  refuses a table that misses it by more than 0.08.
- **The curve's numbers.** Toe −1.1, Latitude 1.2 with white at 0, γ 1.2 at
  Development 0.5 (0.6–2.4 geometric), fog 0.004–0.030. Chosen so a red at 7% of
  white sits below the toe and a sky at 80% on the shoulder; inside the published
  picture of a high-contrast, short-latitude material, not fitted to a plate.
- **Frames are weighted by seconds.** A bucket holds Σ(exposure × dt), dt from the
  host's clock clamped to [1/240, 1/4] s, the first frame a nominal 1/60. A paused
  host clock therefore adds nothing; a real plate would keep exposing, but the
  fleet's contract is that time is the host's.
- **Buckets ≤ 16**, because the sum pass binds one sampler per bucket and sixteen
  units per stage is OpenGL 4.1's guarantee. 16 buckets at 4K is 570 MB.
- **An Exposure change keeps the ring;** a Buckets or Mode change resets it.
- **Take is edge-triggered** on the value crossing 0.5; in Continuous mode the
  button does nothing. Switching to Take mode caps the lens (the plate goes blank
  until a take); switching back opens it.
- **A take stops at elapsed ≥ Exposure − 1e-9**, so the frame exactly Exposure
  after the event is the first held frame.
- **The coating is cached** by a key of everything it depends on, so a still plate
  costs nothing there; it is recomputed on a resize.
- **Defects are seeded at 1**, no seed control: fifteen names is enough for a
  first release.
- **Output alpha is 1.** See the trap.
- **Test hooks live in the shipped plugin** (the `Perturb` bits in the shader and the
  CPU, `Probe` in the plate pass), always zero.
- **No factory presets, no user guide** (`guide=""`), no browser demo, no OpenFX.

## What is actually verified, and what is assumed

### Verified by measurement, on an M4 Max running macOS 26.4 (2026-09-24)

Every number is `tools/verify.sh` on this machine against a fresh universal Release
build, at 320×180 and 1280×720.

- **Spectral.** Under each light, red/green/blue over white equal the computed
  weights to 3e-6; white 1.0000001. Daylight: red 0.0737, green 0.0162, blue 0.9802.
- **Ghost.** 78-column smear, every column (frames covered)/30 to 2e-6; static 1;
  black exactly 0; the window holds 6 buckets, 0.500000 s.
- **Bucket.** Worst difference from the box mean 0.1667 = the bound, reached; the
  stated rule to 2e-6; the window length right on all 31 frames.
- **Curve.** At γ 0.6, 1.2, 2.4: every step within 6.2e-7 of the curve; floors
  0.00400/0.01700/0.03000 (fog); ceilings fog + γL within 2e-4; secants 0.56/1.12/2.24
  in their bands.
- **Drainage.** All four pours: residual 1.8e-7; a = 0.20000, b = 1.20000.
- **Take.** Blank before; 29 of 29 changes; 41 of 41 held frames bit-identical.
- **Resize.** Worst 0.00e+00 against 0.5, 4 buckets, 1.000000 s.
- **Negative controls.** All seven fail their check.
- **Mutation.** Caught by `--drainage` (above).
- **No dead controls**, all 15, with the four About buttons skipped.
- **Every shader compiles** through `glslc`, as the plugin assembles it; no reserved
  word as an identifier.
- **The weights recompute** byte for byte.
- **`--pipe`** returns exactly two frames for two and a half, refuses an unknown cue,
  exits 1 on a closed stdout (`| head -c 1`) and on a failed render, steps an option
  cue and fires an event cue on its frame.
- **The bundle** is universal, exports `_plugMain`, carries
  `com.stoatworks.ffgl.wetplate`, ad-hoc signs, and `oxbow` reports `SW Wetplate` /
  `WT01` / `effect` and renders 120 frames through `plugMain`.
- **Render cost** at the defaults, best of three runs of 60 frames after a warm-up,
  `glFinish` both sides, the card uploaded once, on a shared GPU:

  | | ms/frame | % of a 60fps frame | state held (8 buckets) |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1280×720 | 0.12 | 0.7% | 35 MB |
  | 1920×1080 | 0.31 | 1.9% | 79 MB |
  | 3840×2160 | 1.25 | 7.5% | 316 MB |

  State is (Buckets + 2) R32F frames, colour textures only; the SDK's FBO also
  attaches a depth renderbuffer to each, which the plugin never uses.

### Assumed, or not done

- ☠️ **Never loaded into Resolume.** Everything numeric was compiled, rendered and
  measured offline against the real plugin class in a headless CGL context, plus an
  `oxbow` load. Windows has never been compiled.
- **Never seen on camera footage.** The pictures are the synthetic card and eight of
  Resolume's bundled demo clips (CG loops) through `wttest --pipe`. Skin, foliage,
  sky — where a blue-blind plate shows most — are unjudged.
- **The clock-unit voting** is readout's, which has met Arena; this plugin has not.
- **The bare edge and the comets are judged by eye** on the demo clips and nothing
  checks them beyond the sweep.
- **The presentation colours** (silver, plate, light, warm ends) are chosen by eye.
- **Not verified at 4K**, only benchmarked there.
- **The 320×180 sweep is the only check of Light, Tone, Vignette, Bare Edge and
  Defects**: they change the picture; nothing measures how.
- **`StoatworksAbout.h` and `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` are provisional hand copies.**
- **Nothing has been through a show.**

## Open questions

- **Should a paused host clock keep exposing?** A real plate would. The fleet's
  contract makes time the host's, so a paused clip adds nothing to the plate.
- **Should the ring rebucket on an Exposure change** rather than let the old grid's
  slots fall out by the rule? The rule is correct but the window is briefly
  irregular after a big change.
- **Is half a second the right default?** It suits the demo clips; a slower, stiller
  subject wants seconds, and the slider goes there.
- **The UV.** The plate's sensitivity below 380 nm is real and unrepresentable from
  RGB. A `UV` slider adding a fraction of the blue channel would be a fudge; left out.
- **Grain.** Collodion is fine, but a tintype revival print at 4K might show some.
- **A Seed control** for the pour edge and the defects, so two layers differ.
